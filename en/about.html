About

Why this site exists

I come from the libertarian movement. Everyone there talks about Bitcoin, but almost no one can explain what a hash function is, why SHA-256, or what mining actually means. The result is a community that defends something it doesn't understand.

This site exists to fix that, on the technical and conceptual side, from zero.

Who writes this

I'm Renato Diniz, a Brazilian software engineer. I've worked with Rust for a few years, in distributed systems, infrastructure, and high-volume telemetry pipelines.

Studying Bitcoin in depth made me realize that even the most militant part of the libertarian community rarely gets past slogans. That's where this site comes from.

More on GitHub.

Why two tracks

Because two kinds of people want to understand Bitcoin and both are being poorly served. Those looking for concepts get slogans. Those looking for code get abstractions.

Here both tracks start on the same trunk (economics, history of money) and then diverge. You pick which one to follow, or follow both.

About the content

Open, free, bilingual (Portuguese and English). No newsletter, no paywall, no affiliates.

If you spot something wrong, open a PR on the project's GitHub. Better than complaining.
